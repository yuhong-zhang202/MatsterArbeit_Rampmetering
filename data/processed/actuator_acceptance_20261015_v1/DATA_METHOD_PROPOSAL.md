## 3. Boundary, delay and accuracy are different budgets

For selected nominal2/T exposure on intact consecutive packets, the integral equals2 on each full packet. An arbitrary window cuts two packets: let f_start/f_end be their uncompleted accrued fractions in[0,2). Then C_selected(W)−E_sched(W)=f_end−f_start, so its magnitude is<2 vehicles under these assumptions; it is not two independent2-vehicle allowances to add automatically. At H activation the initial fraction is0. Preserve exact fractions; held application, different execution support and failure tails do not inherit this simple bound. Completed event belongs to W iff a<end<=b, with W=[a,b); this cumulative-difference convention intentionally includes a packet finishing at b and excludes one finishing at a. N uses interval-begin assignment; their unequal support is explicit, not mistaken for lost vehicles.

Request application delay is a different term. For one300↔900 step delayed at most24s, the request-versus-delayed-request integral can differ by600*24/3600=4vehicles, before physical discharge/packet boundary effects. The bound follows only for one uninterrupted eligible delayed step, not many updates or holds. A30s Feedback interval may be split by a packet boundary;300s contains10 Feedback intervals and12.5–37.5 nominal8–24s packet lengths, not ten independent rate samples. Record exact integral of original rate minus its delayed applied-request rate using request IDs (a separate diagnostic from existing nominal C_applied=2/T); do not conflate those two applied meanings. At each boundary verify the declared selection and unchanged ALINEA prior state. Do not silently add4vehicles to every window, remove a transient window, or charge it to a defective device.

Consequently the proposed actual tracking screen below can reject a mathematically correct but too-slow/too-coarse policy after a large valid command transition. That means **accuracy specification not met**, not hardware/API corruption or permission to alter safety. An exact nominal/lifecycle PASS may coexist with actual precision failure. If scientific review considers that precision target too demanding for the existing catalogue, amend the Proposed criterion before any new results; do not retrospectively forgive it. No paper supplies a universal compatible tolerance. Technical/safety qualification and scientific usefulness remain separate.

## 4. One numeric recommendation and decision rule

Recommend **B(W)=max(2 vehicles,0.05*C_W)**, two-sided |N_W−C_W|<=B(W), for every complete opportunity window and their combined eligible support. At300s,2vehicles=24veh/h; at target300/600/900 this envelope is2/2.5/3.75vehicles (24/30/45veh/h). Two vehicles allow one complete nominal packet of boundary discreteness;5% caps accumulated bias rather than permitting a fixed packet loss repeatedly. These values are proposed engineering usefulness targets, not fitted to run02 and not the historical10% screen. Integer resolution means the low-rate screen can exceed5%; disclose it instead of calling it uniformly5% accuracy.

For H and the six-window union, always apply/report the SAME formula as **raw delivery target MET/NOT_MET/PARTIAL**, even when externally limited. That raw result is not itself an attribution of actuator fault. Conditional accuracy can be ACCEPTED_ON_OBSERVED_OPPORTUNITY only if minimum opportunity coverage exists, all eligible windows and their aggregate meet B, and no unresolved persistent loss/lifecycle/integrity/safety blocker exists. A complete opportunity window outside B is NOT_ACCEPTABLE_ACCURACY. Only external/unknown windows cannot establish conditional accuracy (NOT_TESTED), even if a raw error happens to be small. Mixed-support H does not acquire a blanket 'C tracked' PASS from its eligible subset; full-horizon qualification must state the external limitations explicitly. It is impossible to promise raw C=N at all times under unavailable demand without changing the target/controller; this proposal does neither.

**Persistent underdischarge:** two adjacent complete opportunity windows each d>B and their combined d>B(combined) constitute sustained unresolved shortfall; flag NOT_ACCEPTABLE_ACCURACY and retain all residuals. A single failed eligible window already fails its accuracy test; the persistence flag adds duration evidence, not permission to ignore that failure. Compute overrelease symmetrically, do not permit later excess to erase earlier window failure. No correction command is generated from C−N. Existing safe STOP behavior and posthoc accuracy decision remain separate; a retrospective accuracy failure does not itself authorize runtime hard-stop changes.

# Current-version V15 exploratory matched three-seed synthesis

**Scope and status.** This is an exploratory comparison at M3600/R900, seeds 17, 23 and 42, fixed 4200 s horizon and `[1200,3000)` evaluation. The formal protocol is empty/unfrozen. Three pairs are the analysis units; windows, detector records and vehicles are not independent replications. This report does not establish a feasible sweet spot.

## Data gate and method

The independent [S17](../stage6_safe_actuator_v15_audit_20261003_v1/S17_GATE_REVIEW.md), [S23](../stage6_safe_actuator_s23_v15_audit_20261003_v1/S23_GATE_REVIEW.md) and [S42](../stage6_safe_actuator_s42_v15_audit_20261003_v1/S42_GATE_REVIEW.md) technical/data gates all pass. Each V15 run is 4200 continuous seconds; first 600 FCD seconds match its own OPEN seed exactly; all 4050 requested M/R/U/X vehicles are inserted and arrived in both arms, with no unfinished or undeparted vehicles. Every fixed six-window rate screen passes with continuously eligible storage queue and no excluded window. Raw identities are SHA-bound in the three gate reviews and processed `summary.json` files. OPEN sources are `data/processed/stage6_boundary_search_20261002_v1/M3600_R900_S{seed}/`; V15 sources are the three gate directories above. The source demand is identical within each seed. Full requested-class **observed system seconds** include external source waiting from scheduled departure and are paired by seed. No run, vehicle or window was dropped.

## Interpretation boundary and handoff

**Confirmed:** V15 executes its commanded metering rates and improves all three paired mainline states at this demand while imposing large R and U system-time costs. R slow queues reach the shared road and U is sometimes slow behind R during city green. All six paired runs finish, so these class totals have no end-of-run vehicle censoring. **Supported but limited inference:** the physical queue and same-lane co-occurrence make ramp-to-urban interference plausible under V15, alongside the large measured source waiting. The paired design supports a policy-associated contrast at this one demand, not a universal causal decomposition of U delay. **Unresolved:** no qualifying ≥30 s continuous meter-to-shared physical chain, no demonstration that a less restrictive standard setting achieves mainline benefit with acceptable R/U costs, and no formal protocol or adequate demand/seed coverage for a sweet-spot claim. Do not add simulations solely because possible; research lead/scientific reviewer should decide whether the exploratory target requires the strict chain and whether these already observed phenomena suffice for closure.

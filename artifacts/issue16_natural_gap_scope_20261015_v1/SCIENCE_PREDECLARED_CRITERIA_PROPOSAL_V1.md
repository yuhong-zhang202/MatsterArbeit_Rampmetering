# Proposed preregistered qualification boundary V1

Recorded by primary from independent scientific_reviewer response; proposal only, not a user-approved method decision, formal protocol or execution permission.

Seven domains are minimum evidence gates. Explainable constraints do not automatically establish accurate service.

- TECHNICAL_PASS: independently consistent ACK, signal, request/application, exact C/E/N, partial/pending, population, EOF and version binding. Numeric representation only follows existing precision, not a new physical tolerance.
- NOT_QUALIFIED: real hard anomaly, required safety coverage UNKNOWN, inconsistent accounting, or persistent unexplained service loss when actual service opportunities exist. E, constraint labels or end-of-run cannot replace N.
- NOT_TESTED: unencountered branches such as constrained recovery, insufficient service opportunity and untested capacity. Numeric tracking precision without a predefined acceptance scale remains NOT_ASSESSED.
- Limited operational/measurement qualification: only after4200s, allsix windows and seven domains, no unresolved required safety/data issue, and actual service evidence adequate for the declared purpose. Final scientific judgment required; one seed covers only that card.

Retain allsix windows, cumulative trajectories and C-N components. Supply, receiving, start-up, discrete cycles and application delay need their own evidence. Instantaneous no-vehicle/receiver denial cannot explain a whole-window deficit. Physical constraints are not recoverable debt and still restrict useful capacity. Labeling deficits does not mechanically yield PASS.

Single method decision for the exact-card approval: whether the user accepts only limited operational/measurement qualification for this run, keeping numeric ALINEA tracking accuracy NOT_ASSESSED. If the goal includes certified sufficient accuracy, a purpose-based actual deviation tolerance must be justified and approved before running; current sources do not justify5%,10% or a fixed vehicle count. The approved safety contract does not approve an accuracy standard.

Source scope: already-reviewed Issue14 seven domains, Issue16 T3 and existing literature review. No new literature/matrix/test/native operation performed for this proposal.

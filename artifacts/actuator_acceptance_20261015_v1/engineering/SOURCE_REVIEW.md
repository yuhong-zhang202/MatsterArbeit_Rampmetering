## 3. What the epsilon calculation does and does not establish

Let Δ be motion step seconds, h the stopping calculation's headway seconds, b desired deceleration m/s², and εx=0.001m the distance subtraction. For one fixed Euler formula branch n, the stopping speed is `n bΔ + (gap−εx−Hn)/(nΔ+h)`. Relative to the same expression without that subtraction, `δv=−εx/(nΔ+h)` and `δa=−εx/[Δ(nΔ+h)]`. Branch changes, caps and the emergency wrapper are separate conditions; this is not a global derivative through those discontinuities.

For n=0, h=actionStep=Δ and the conditional stop-distance boundary equal to the one-braking-step Euler brakeGap, `bΔ<v<2bΔ`, brakeGap=`(v−bΔ)Δ`; hence `v_next=v−bΔ−εx/Δ`, and `a=−b−εx/Δ²`. At Δ=1s the scale is 0.001m/s². This gives a source-compatible scale and an externally checkable conditional pattern. It does not identify the inaccessible actual stopDist/branch, prove every −4.501 event numerical, or authorize a universal acceleration allowance.

POSITION_EPS=0.1m clamps a stop-line offset; it is not a collision clearance or 0.1m allowed overlap. The intended 0.001m subtraction is different from IEEE floating-point roundoff. Precision8 FCD representation has about 5e−9 units of last-decimal rounding per recorded value; that representation error does not explain a 0.001m/s² excursion. Euler/ballistic integration error is another, model-dependent quantity. The existing 1e−6 comparison tolerance checks equality/representation consistency; it must not be renamed physical validation.

## 4. Single recommended graded policy — PROPOSED, not active

Retain the ordinary desired-deceleration gate and every unrelated hard safety/UNKNOWN gate. Add only a narrow **NUMERICAL_SCALE_COMPATIBLE** observation candidate for the first yellow step on the actuator's controlled storage lane, for any fully bound vehicle, route and seed satisfying the same declared model/context. This is intentionally not an exemption on all roads/phases. Other-lane, ordinary following, green/red, unsupported model/integration/actionStep events remain outside this proposed diagnostic scope; their STOP does not itself prove physical danger.

Candidate eligibility is conjunctive:

1. Exact approved card/source/config bind Krauss, semi-implicit Euler, Δ=actionStep=1s; before/after effective dynamics and modes are unchanged and finite. Desired deceleration remains the measured normal value; emergency is recorded only as context.
2. Peak excess over desired deceleration is positive and ≤`εx/Δ² + 1e−6 m/s²`; raw getter and Δv peaks remain recorded. The existing strict FAIL is retained alongside the new label. Values below the original comparison tolerance need no exception.
3. Externally observed stopping context: the same vehicle stays on the bound storage lane across that first-yellow step, without entering the controlled link; lane geometry/position/signal link/program/phase clocks are bound. Its speed lies in the one-braking-step domain, inferred offset `lane_end−x_before−brakeGap(v_before,b,0)` lies within the fixed source's applicable stop-offset range `[POSITION_EPS, declared/default stop offset]`, and the conditional n=0 speed/displacement calculation matches within the existing representation tolerance. Log these as compatibility witnesses; do not claim that inferred offset equals private native stopDist.
4. Necessary before/after world, body, same-lane leader and follower identities (or explicitly observed absence through a known lane boundary), path gaps and finite states are present. No physical overlap, minGap infringement, red entry, collision/teleport, concurrent excessive braking outside this narrow candidate, phase/ACK mismatch, unexpected native warning/error or other hard fault is present. Previously approved passive current-reference insufficiency remains visible and cannot on its own supply this no-hazard evidence. Missing required neighbor coverage is UNKNOWN/STOP, not assumed clear. Inaccessible private branch identity is a causal limitation, not an automatic missing-observation failure.

Only under future explicit approval, a qualifying event could permit **one unchanged native motion step** for follow-up. All other safety gates and observation duties remain active before/after that step; no TLS, vehicle parameter or velocity is changed to obtain recovery. Reconcile that step against real ACK and recheck the vehicle, neighbors, body/gaps, signals, native counters/logs and acceleration. Any new excessive-braking event while follow-up is pending, missing observation or hard fault stops; continuation is not renewed repeatedly. Successful follow-up closes only this observation episode, not safety or qualification proof. Subsequent separate episodes, including the same vehicle after closure, are each evaluated under the entire predicate and individually logged/reviewed; they cannot be averaged away or called normal by event frequency alone. For the global all-vehicle safety chain, vehicles outside this narrow location/phase continue under the original rule.

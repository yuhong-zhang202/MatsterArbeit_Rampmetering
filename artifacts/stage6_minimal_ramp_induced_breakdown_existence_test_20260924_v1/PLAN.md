# MINIMAL_RAMP_INDUCED_BREAKDOWN_EXISTENCE_TEST

**Status:** `PROPOSED_PLAN_ONLY — NO_RUN_AUTHORIZATION`  
**Scope:** Stage 6 exploratory mechanism-existence diagnostic, 2026-09-24. No simulation, executable card, scientific input, classifier, threshold, old witness-contract amendment, or formal-protocol change is authorized by this plan.

## 1. Question and inference boundary

Can the already validated freeway/ramp network, with urban background classes U and X temporarily set to zero, produce a sustained collective mainline deterioration after **actual** delayed ramp traffic reaches the merge, while its matched R=0 control does not show sustained mainline congestion? This is a modular diagnostic of whether an R-related freeway mechanism exists in the synthetic scenario. It is not a capacity estimate, a pure merge-friction proof, a calibrated qMain choice, or evidence that metering helps the complete urban network.

The repaired full-network PAIR3199 sensitivity analysis stopped at Gate B as `NOT_EVALUABLE`: M/U/X matched before R activation, but seven U identities diverged on the shared source/approach after t=540 and the source/insertion/RNG alternative remained unresolved. No later P/S/L or witness outcome was opened. This new module does **not** reinterpret that raw, amend its witness contract, or turn its historical result into a negative or positive finding.

Robert Hilbrich's 2026-08-25 reply supports first testing whether a simple synthetic freeway/ramp/urban scenario can produce plausible breakdown, and checking vehicle insertion, demand, merge/lane-change behavior and geometry if it cannot. It does not explicitly endorse U=X=0, these exact demand values, or this run budget. U=X=0 is a proposed diagnostic subtest of that broader objective; the original freeway–ramp–urban trade-off still requires a later complete-network test.

## 4. Prospective evidence gates

1. **Input and pre-treatment matching.** Planned M IDs/departs/routes/types/speedFactors must match 100%; U=X=0 on both sides; only treatment has R192. For t<540, reconcile M identity, actual departures, trajectory coverage and vehicle-time FCD tuples. An unexplained material pre-treatment divergence is `NOT_EVALUABLE`. Do not tune seed/inputs after seeing outcomes.
2. **R=0 control eligibility.** Verify adequate realized M insertion and exposure in merge, upstream and downstream observation cells; complete lifecycle and FCD/E1 coverage; no locked P/S/L sustained event during the fixed relevant period; adjudicate persistent Candidate C/low-speed patterns using their location, density, persistence and recovery. P/S/L-negative alone is insufficient if a relevant, unexplained sustained self-congestion or source/downstream artifact remains. A perfect high-mobility/reference screen is not claimed.
3. **Treatment exposure and chronology.** Reconcile planned/inserted/unfinished M and R, departures and delays. Record R activation, first actual R departure, first approach arrival, auxiliary entry, first through-lane merge, three-consecutive-bin meaningful exposure (the existing T3 measurement convention), first M deterioration and State1 onset as distinct events. Require positive, route-consistent R entry in three complete consecutive 30 s bins and T3 confirmation by t<=720 for this proposed module. Report 1 Hz time-censoring and R admission/TLS platoons; nominal R720 alone is not exposure. If R does not meaningfully enter, result is `NOT_EVALUABLE`, not `NO_WITNESS`.
4. **Mainline event and matched attribution.** Keep locked P/S/L and Candidate A/C definitions exactly as they are. A fully qualifying locked P event is the principal positive; a fully qualifying S event with independently coherent M spatial/trajectory evidence may be reviewed separately. Require onset **after** meaningful R exposure, first qualifying low bin in the predeclared `[720,1440)` primary window, and confirmation before 1500 while R continues to enter. Compare treatment and control at identical time, lane and cell; require sustained collective M deterioration not equivalently present in control. Candidate C alone, isolated low speeds, or lower detector flow alone do not qualify. Report all [0,2700) observations, including negative or earlier/later events; do not move the window after viewing them.
5. **Alternative causes.** Check M source starvation/insertion delay, R source starvation or excess queueing, downstream receiving space/tailback, fixed TLS states, route/lane mapping, geometry and lane changes, detector/FCD gaps, and unfinished/censored vehicles. A treatment-only upstream source or downstream restriction that could explain the M event makes attribution `NOT_EVALUABLE` until resolved. A positive pair supports an **R-admission/merge-associated** freeway witness; claiming specifically merge friction requires the spatial and timing evidence to exclude source/TLS effects.

Allowed diagnostic outcomes for this module: `RAMP_INDUCED_FREEWAY_WITNESS_ESTABLISHED` (exploratory, bounded); `NO_WITNESS_WITHIN_BOUNDED_MODULE` after all eligible permitted pairs are genuinely negative; or `NOT_EVALUABLE` with the failed gate and minimal repair stated. These are new module labels and do not overwrite old PAIR3199 histories.

# PHENOMENON_LEVEL_ABC_GATE

**Status:** `PROPOSED_STAGE6_EXPLORATORY_ONLY` — formulated after observing the existing Stage 6 outcomes. This is a purpose-specific scenario plausibility gate, not a reclassification of historical runs, an executable run card, a controller setting, or a formal experiment protocol. Stage 6 remains `PARTIAL` until the full chain is supported and independently reviewed.

## Scientific basis and limits

Simulation validity depends on the intended use and the required output behaviour, while data validity and implementation verification remain distinct checks ([Sargent 2013](https://doi.org/10.1057/jos.2012.20)). Pattern-oriented modelling evaluates a model against multiple observable patterns at relevant scales rather than a single output label ([Grimm et al. 2005](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1116681); [Grimm & Railsback 2012](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2011.0180)). Traffic microsimulation verification and validation also require documented behavioural and technical checks ([Reinke et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.3141/1876-16)). **Inference for this project:** Stage 6 can ask whether the linked freeway, ramp and urban responses occur in a controlled synthetic scenario without making a confirmed State1 breakdown the sole admission ticket. Pattern-oriented modelling evaluates patterns observed in a target system; here it inspires an internal synthetic-scenario plausibility check and does not independently validate the model against real traffic. These publications do not validate this project's qMain/qRamp choices, a 60 s cutoff, the 0.85 reference, or a one-seed causal claim.

## What the gate evaluates

Use a matched, internally comparable **full-network** A/B/C set to evaluate the whole chain. The controller labels are **A=open**, **B=moderate metering**, **C=stronger metering**. The three patterns are:

1. **Pressure and local mainline response (phenomenon A):** Under an open ramp, higher *realized* R merge exposure is associated with local M deterioration that is temporally sustained within the observed run relative to a lower-R matched comparison. Record the first R departure/auxiliary/through entry, accumulation through the candidate window, first M difference and onset. Show M-only changes at fixed times, lanes and cells with vehicle counts, speed and concentration/trajectory context. The observation must extend beyond an isolated one-bin or one-vehicle warning. A practical candidate is at least two adjacent complete 30 s bins in a fixed merge-area location, consistent with the already documented 60 s L diagnostic; this is an **exploratory convention**, disclosed as post-outcome for the present evidence, not a calibrated traffic constant. A failed 0.85 onset reference is retained and prevents a *clean onset/State1* claim, but does not erase directly measured sustained deterioration.
2. **Moderate metering response (phenomenon B):** At the **same full-network demand, M/R/U/X input definitions, seed, geometry and observation horizon** as A, B must show a credible M improvement relative to A on convergent **M-only** measures: a fixed-location/time lane-cell speed and population/density comparison plus a consistent M travel-time, delay or passage measure. Reduced mixed-class E1 flow caused by suppressing R is not M protection. Report the B ramp queue length/count/duration and U cohort costs with insertion wait and censoring separate. “Acceptable ramp cost” requires a predeclared storage/urban interpretation; a near-zero U mean difference alone is not an equivalence test.
3. **Stronger metering cost (phenomenon C):** At the same matched full-network condition, C must create clearly larger R storage and/or internal/shared occupation than B, together with larger U cost in a complete-cohort accounting that separates external insertion wait, network residence and unfinished vehicles. The ordering of controller restriction, R buildup and U deterioration must be documented. Observed co-occurrence supports a cost pattern; a claim of physical queue spillback requires separate spatial blockage evidence. The *trade-off* is established only when B's M benefit and C's R/U cost belong to this same comparable A/B/C chain.

## Minimum quantitative evidence, without formal calibration

- For every comparison, freeze the population, exact input differences, seed, time interval, mapped lanes/cells and denominator **before interpreting that comparison**. Show full candidate and non-candidate windows, not only the most favourable episode. For future observations, register the gate before looking at outcomes; the current offline audit is explicitly retrospective.
- Report actual planned/inserted/arrived/unfinished M/R/U/X identities, departure and merge exposure, controller TLS states, 1 s trajectories and complete 30 s aggregates. Publish absolute and normalized M speed, simultaneous slow-vehicle/population counts, local density or occupancy context, ramp queue extent/count/duration, and U wait/residence including censoring. Quantify direction, magnitude, duration and measurement precision; keep interval bounds where the detector or FCD cannot resolve an exact time.
- Require more than one mutually informative pattern for each claimed mechanism: M speed plus population/spatial structure for A or B, and R queue/occupation plus U cost for C. A single vehicle's minimum speed, a mixed E1 passage count, a plot without raw counts, or an unbounded mean is insufficient. A fixed one-seed observation can demonstrate a possible pattern in this model; it cannot establish frequency, robustness across seeds, real-world validity or an optimum.
- Fail closed for missing raw/provenance, mismatched exogenous demand, unrealized R merge exposure, source starvation, unreported unfinished vehicles, wrong lane mapping, detector coverage gaps, geometry inconsistency, direct TLS/downstream restriction, and material pre-treatment trajectory differences. Post-treatment source-region or RNG/order feedback is **not automatically exogenous confounding**, but its possible effect on a merge-specific claim must be bounded and reported.
- Keep the locked P/S/L, Candidate A/C and 0.85 reference outputs beside the phenomenon assessment. Do not change their thresholds, historical `NO_WITNESS`/`NOT_EVALUABLE` labels, or use a phenomenon-level result as a State1 or capacity-drop claim. Label each leg `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_IDENTIFIED`, or `NOT_EVALUABLE` with reasons; no numeric score or parameter ranking.

**Stage 6 acceptance:** only after A, B and C are supported within one scientifically comparable full-network chain, with technical/artifact checks passed and no material alternative explanation for the *claimed* mechanism. A minimal U=X=0 pressure result may support A's plausibility but cannot fill B or the urban part of C. The strict State1 classifier remains a diagnostic sensitivity check; a confirmed State1 event is not required for this **proposed exploratory phenomenon gate**. Any later formal claim about preventing breakdown requires a separately approved, validated formal outcome definition and study design.

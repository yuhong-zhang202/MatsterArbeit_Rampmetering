# Stage 6 A random-driving sensitivity diagnostic

**Status:** prospective technical diagnostic design; independent prelaunch review required. It is separate from the previously bounded default-model quartet. It does not reclassify the historical `A_NOT_EVALUABLE`, the new default-model A attribution hold, or any P/S/L result.

## Problem

The new default-model R0/A pair is statically and pre-R matched. A has slower M traffic in fixed core cells 14/15 and modestly longer M trips. Yet the first four diverging M at 594 s are 45–234 m ahead of the first R, and at 599 s 22 M upstream of x1000 m already differ although the first R reached the merge through lane only at 592 s near x1426 m. The simultaneous distant changes are not a plausible physical wave from that merge. SUMO 1.26's default Krauß `sigma=0.5` uses stochastic driving-speed perturbations. A local source-code investigation suggests that vehicle driving RNG can depend on its current lane, so adding R may shift shared random draws; this exact mechanism has not been proven for the observed vehicles. The exact share of the observed A deterioration due to any stochastic coupling is unknown. The corrected A report is `a_attribution/A_OUTCOME_REPORT_REV2.md` and its old provisional report remains preserved.

## One bounded paired sensitivity

Run **exactly two** new diagnostic arms, `R0_SIGMA0` then `A_SIGMA0`, with the same 3350.4/R900 delayed 540–1500 s M1396/R240/U150/X75 demand, prospective per-ID speedFactor vector, requested `best/max/last`, network, route topology, TLS OPEN, seed17, 1 s step, 2700 s horizon and detectors as the default-model V2 package. The **only scientific behavior change** is a new M-only vType copied from `technical_passenger` with `sigma="0"`, assigned to every M in both arms; R/U/X remain on the original `technical_passenger` default. This suppresses the M Krauß random speed perturbation while retaining R and urban behavior as closely as possible. Other lane-changing/RNG channels remain possible, and M following/capacity may change. It is a deliberate altered-model technical sensitivity, not a new thesis controller/parameter candidate, a valid replacement R0/A observation, or an ALINEA test. No other sigma value, seed, demand, geometry, controller adjustment or B/C diagnostic is in scope.

Build both arms before either starts. Hash-bind their full demand, network, type, config, detectors, SUMO binary, local schemas and runner; show demand XML differs from the default arms only by adding the M-only type and changing M vehicle `type` references. Use new immutable input/raw/processed directories, separate one-use cards and a 120 s / 100 MB / 100 ms resource contract per arm. A technical failure requires a versioned environment-only fix and fresh engineering/data/scientific review; it does not authorize a different behavior or repeated scientific attempt. No old raw is overwritten.

## Prospective gates and measures

1. R0_SIGMA0: verify complete 2700 s/outputs, M/U/X planned/inserted/arrived/unfinished and pre-R source behavior. Do not infer a clean baseline.
2. A_SIGMA0: before any post-R M interpretation, demand and `[0,540)` actual M/U/X insertion and exact FCD trajectory equality versus the new R0_SIGMA0 must pass. Verify R through-lane exposure, insertion and lifecycle. Failure stops analysis.
3. For the matched pair, show **every** fixed 30 s M-only core cell13–17 bin over `[0,2700)`, especially `[540,1500)` and the late tail, M speed plus simultaneous population/density, all-M trip duration/timeLoss and passage. Reparse raw identities. Trip duration/timeLoss do not include external pre-insertion waiting: report departDelay, never-inserted, unfinished and internal ramp/urban queue coverage separately. Examine exact/common M trajectory differences at **every second 592–605 s**, including ahead-of-first-R vehicles and x<1000 m as a predeclared nonlocal negative-control region; provide complete IDs and x<1000/x<1400 counts, explicitly including t594 and t599. Report when/where first differences occur without using first-divergence time alone as a causal onset. Compare the direction/persistence of cell14/15 differences and R spatial encounters with the default pair, disclosing changed R trajectory/exposure and behavior.

Interpretation is **asymmetric**: if the predeclared early nonlocal counts fall to zero while a localized sustained M response remains after R physically arrives, that strengthens physical-mechanism plausibility but does not measure the default-run causal share or prove its A leg. If nonlocal differences persist, attribution remains unresolved. If both nonlocal and local effects disappear, the diagnostic is ambiguous because the driving model changed. These are project diagnostic rules, not literature thresholds. No result alone releases B or closes Stage 6; an independent scientific review must decide if the default-model A evidence plus this sensitivity is sufficient for exploratory A, or whether Stage 6 remains partial. Keep single-seed, synthetic and non-ALINEA limits explicit.

**Basis:** SUMO documents separate RNG categories, default Krauß stochastic speed variation at `sigma=0.5`, and the model's sensitivity to insertion ([SUMO randomness](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Simulation/Randomness.html)). This is an engineering isolation judgment, not a published numerical traffic criterion. Simulation verification and operational validity are separate in [Sargent 2013](https://doi.org/10.1057/jos.2012.20); the new run cannot substitute for real-traffic calibration or a formal multi-seed alternative analysis as discussed in [FHWA 2019](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwahop18036/chapter6.htm).

# Literature and official mechanism basis

Checked 2026-10-15. Short paraphrases only. Evidence levels below refer to text actually accessed in this task; an engineering implementation is not attributed to a paper unless the paper actually specifies it. No source gives the present SUMO network a service or safety certificate.

## Required literature

- **Papageorgiou & Papamichail (2008), Overview of Traffic Signal Operation Policies for Ramp Metering**, DOI10.3141/2047-04. [Publisher abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3141/2047-04) and [TRB record](https://trid.trb.org/View/847664) read; publisher explicitly shows restricted full access. Supports distinguishing rate-control strategy from signal execution policy and identifies several implementation families. No full-text timing recommendation or empirical two-car guarantee is claimed.
- **Kotsialos, Papageorgiou, Hayden, Higginson, McCabe & Rayman (2006), Discrete release rate impact on ramp metering performance**, DOI10.1049/ip-its:20055003. [Author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/3465157_Discrete_release_rate_impact_on_ramp_metering_performance), methods§2.1/2.2/3.3 read. ALINEA uses its own clipped prior request; the discussed stationary error property assumes inactive constraints and sufficient ramp demand. Discrete implementation can differ from physical release. The study uses METANET, so it does not calibrate this microscopic green/yellow catalogue, prove two vehicles per cycle, or justify chasing accumulated physical shortfall. No wholesale paper reproduction or copied numeric recommendation.
- **FHWA Ramp Management and Control Handbook**, [chapter5 flow control§5.3.6](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/ramp_mgmt_handbook/manual/manual/5_1.htm), [chapter6 strategy selection](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/ramp_mgmt_handbook/manual/manual/6_1.htm), [chapter10 detection](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/ramp_mgmt_handbook/manual/manual/10_1.htm), official relevant sections read. Demand/passage detectors support presence-sensitive release and observed passage; waiting red without demand is ordinary operation. Freeway, ramp and arterial impacts must remain visible. Handbook capacities/timings are contextual and are not adopted as present SUMO limits.

## SUMO official mechanisms

- [Traffic Lights](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Simulation/Traffic_Lights.html), [TraCI setters](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/TraCI/Change_Traffic_Lights_State.html) and [getters](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/TraCI/Traffic_Lights_Value_Retrieval.html): relevant control sections read. Predefined phases and remaining-duration changes require real program/phase/state/nextSwitch and setter/readback checks; controlling a phase is distinct from observing a crossing. Extending an already-red phase must preserve preceding clearance. APIs do not guarantee a physical quota.
- [Safety](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Simulation/Safety.html): collision detection/action, signal-related collision mechanisms and reaction-time sections read. Short clearance and disabled vehicle safeguards can invalidate safety; retain collision, red-entry and braking monitoring and original physical parameters. A model query is not a universal future-motion proof. Safety outputs and the configured detection domain must be checked, not inferred from a clean CLI exit.
- [Vehicle Insertion](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Simulation/VehicleInsertion.html) and [TripInfo](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Simulation/Output/TripInfo.html): relevant insertion/delayed-departure/unfinished-output sections read. Requested vehicles can wait outside the network; departed, arrived, still-running and undeparted cohorts must reconcile. Arrival-only tripinfo is insufficient for a truncated run; unfinished handling must be explicit.
- [Vehicle getters](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/TraCI/Vehicle_Value_Retrieval.html): follow/secure-gap/stop queries return model- and input-dependent values. Preserve exact joint inputs and existing safety semantics; do not convert low discharge or a query refusal into a proved crash or actuator malfunction.

Current official pages describe mechanisms; the runtime is pinned separately to the installed SUMO1.26.0 binary/libraries/configuration. Website revision dates and current defaults are not substituted for actual getters/configured values.

## Engineering inference to review

The smallest proposed change reuses existing ALINEA and signal phases, corrects only request-to-nominal scheduling quantization, and records physical release separately. The candidate selector uses rawC minus **scheduled nominal opportunities** E_scheduled, not C minus observedN. A denied/empty complete nominal cycle still has scheduled opportunity count, explicitly not supplied service. Separate E_executed, actualG acknowledgement, selected/applied integrals, pending/superseded instructions, and actualN prevent false application claims. C−N=(C−E_scheduled)+(E_scheduled−N) stays visible without allowing the physical term to drive catch-up. This decomposition and selector are project engineering choices, not a quotation from the literature.

Normal absence, observed receiving constraint and startup/following state are evidence categories; missing inputs, broken ACK/phase traces or actual unsafe movement remain errors. Unexplained sustained service loss remains a qualification blocker even if the run continues. Do not invent readiness/causal proof or make completeN=2 a prerequisite for usable observations.

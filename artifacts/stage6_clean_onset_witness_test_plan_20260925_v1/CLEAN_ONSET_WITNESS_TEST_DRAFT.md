The aim is one cleanly ordered exploratory onset, not demand optimization or proof of a formal breakdown boundary.

- The matched 3199/R720 treatment inserted/arrived R192 and is a valid `NO_WITNESS`. It reached T3 at 660 s and had an L-only 60 s candidate, not a qualifying P or coherent S event.
- At qMain=3350.4, R900 increased actual merge exposure over R720 but its four P-low bins in core cell15 [1260,1380) lack the three required immediate pre-onset reference bins at or above 0.85. Its locked result remains `NOT_EVALUABLE / ONSET_REFERENCE_UNRESOLVED`. This result does not establish State1 and does not supply a valid negative.

Lower qMain **may** make an eligible pre-onset reference more plausible, but the current 3199 control classification does not guarantee that any future R900/R1080 episode will have one. The immediate three-bin reference is evaluated in the treatment at the observed onset under the unchanged rule; the R=0 control cannot substitute for it.

3. **Exposure:** record R activation, first departure, approach/storage/auxiliary entries, first through-lane entry, T3, and unique R identities at their first **through-lane** entry by t=1440 and t=1500. Require the previously fixed three consecutive positive complete auxiliary E1 bins with T3 by t<=720. **Before A's prelaunch release or launch**, derive and freeze this unique-first-through-lane-entry method and the two fixed-cutoff comparator counts from the immutable **existing 3199/R720 raw**, with a hash-bound, read-only extraction; its existing review does not report those numbers. A higher nominal R level without strictly higher actual unique merge exposure at both cutoffs ends the plan. No source starvation, TLS admission, downstream receiving-space, censoring, geometry or measurement alternative may materially explain the contrast.
4. **Locked witness:** apply P primary or independently coherent qualifying S under the unchanged method. Preserve L-only and Candidate A/C as diagnostics. Require a qualifying event after meaningful R exposure, first qualifying low bin in the fixed [720,1440) onset window, confirmation before t=1500, and sustained collective M deterioration absent from the existing R=0 control at the same time, cell and lane. A duration-qualified low state with ineligible reference is `ONSET_REFERENCE_UNRESOLVED / NOT_EVALUABLE`, not a witness or `NO_WITNESS`. 5. **Stop:** a valid witness ends demand scanning immediately. ## Alternative: change the R step-onset time at qMain=3350.4

Keep qMain3350.4 while moving or reshaping R admission only under a **separate** versioned prospective design. Changing the step time after seeing the R900 reference failure directly targets the failed eligibility condition and carries stronger result-chasing risk. Moving activation does not ensure that the **immediate** three bins before an eventual M low-state episode will each have speed ratio >=0.85; gradual loading can again make them ineligible. It also changes exposure duration, R arrivals by the fixed cutoffs, and potentially the fixed T3/onset-window gates. Any timing change would require a new causal timing hypothesis, fixed activation/window/count/vector before outputs, renewed matched prelaunch review, and a distinct budget; it cannot be used to relabel existing R900 raw. This plan does **not** select or authorize that alternative.

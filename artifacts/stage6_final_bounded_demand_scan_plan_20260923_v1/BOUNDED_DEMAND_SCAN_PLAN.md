## Question and scientific boundary

The question is whether the accepted synthetic freeway–ramp–urban scene can produce **one** credible exploratory chain: sufficient M supply; no adjudicated sustained mainline congestion at R=0; realized R merge exposure after ramp activation; subsequently a collective, persistent, locally attributable M State1-type event; no evident insertion, downstream, geometry, source, TLS or measurement artifact. If a witness is found, stop demand scanning and separately prepare a fixed A/B/C metering comparison. Neither a witness nor its qMain/qRamp is automatically a formal thesis parameter or a calibrated physical capacity.

The prior Stage 6 validation plan and Luna execution specification require all fixed PRE/control screen rows to PASS before their existing `CONTROL_READY_FOR_TRANSITION_CARD_REVIEW` and `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED_EXPLORATORY` labels. The new user's plan request instead allows a non-perfect high-mobility screen when independent review can establish absence of already sustained R=0 State 1. **These are different proposed eligibility contracts.** This document does not amend the prior files or turn their `CONTROL_NOT_EVALUABLE`/F5 into a PASS. Any future execution using this revised witness contract requires a separate prospective scientific review/approval of the exact contract and card. Until then, no run is released.

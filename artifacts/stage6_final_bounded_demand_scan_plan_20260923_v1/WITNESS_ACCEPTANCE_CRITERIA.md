# Prospective exploratory witness evidence contract — proposed

**Status: PROPOSED, NOT AN AMENDMENT TO THE EARLIER FIXED SCREEN/GATE.** Apply only after independent scientific acceptance and exact future card approval. Preserve the old classifier output and PRE/control screen outcome verbatim. A witnessed event is an exploratory synthetic-scene existence demonstration, not a calibrated physical breakdown, proof of pure merging friction, or formal experiment result.

## 1. Pair integrity and low-R background

For the same qMain, use R0 and delayed-R720 with identical accepted network, M/U/X demand schedule, route, vehicle type/behavior, seed, 1 s step, urban TLS, A_OPEN program, outputs and 0–2700 horizon. Record exact input and source hashes. Compare realized M/U/X speedFactors, departure schedule/delay and pre-R FCD trajectories; same seed is insufficient by itself. Differences caused by changing the R source must be disclosed, and an unexplained pre-R divergence that affects onset attribution makes the pair `NOT_EVALUABLE`.

The low-R arm must have sufficient *observed* M exposure in the merge core, upstream and downstream during the comparison window, full class identity/lifecycle accounting, no established locked P or S State1 episode before or during the relevant event time, and no independently adjudicated sustained collective self-congestion under the existing State0/1/2 evidence dimensions. A P/S/L negative label alone does not prove normality. L/C warnings, long sub-0.85 ratios and failed high-mobility screens must be described and their temporal/spatial/attribution meaning adjudicated; unresolved relevant patterns block acceptance. The low-R arm need not have every reference/normal-screen row PASS under this *new proposed witness contract*. Its old F5 status is retained and cannot be relabelled `CONTROL_READY_FOR_TRANSITION_CARD_REVIEW`.

No obvious downstream receiving-blockage, source starvation, parser defect, incorrect lane mapping, direct TLS restriction or geometry defect may explain the apparent low-R/high-R difference. Absence of an affirmative artifact is weaker than a cleared relevant alternative; unresolved alternatives yield `NOT_EVALUABLE`.

## 2. Actual R merge exposure and chronology

The high-R arm must reconcile scheduled R192 with actual departures, insertion delays, arrival/unfinished status and vehicle IDs, and demonstrate R vehicles actually entering the auxiliary/merge region and interacting with the through lanes. Demand activation at540 s, first R scheduled depart, first actual R departure, first R arrival near merge, first auxiliary entry, first native lane-change/through-lane merge, first **meaningful** merge exposure, first M deterioration, locked State1 onset and later confirmation are distinct markers. Define meaningful exposure prospectively as observed route-consistent R entry in each of three consecutive complete 30 s bins after the first R arrival, with positive *certain* auxiliary-entry counts and observed through-lane entries reported separately; this reuses the prior Luna T3 convention as an exposure check, not an invented flow threshold. **T3 confirmation must occur by t≤720.** Retain 1 Hz interval-censoring and possible/certain boundaries. If T3 cannot be resolved by720, exposure is UNKNOWN for witness eligibility. Confirm that R entry continues across the event; earlier R presence alone does not establish ongoing pressure.

The event's onset interval must start after the physically resolved R arrival and meaningful exposure; if intervals overlap so order cannot be established, classify attribution `NOT_EVALUABLE`. Inspect complete pre-R bins plus the 1 Hz boundary rather than shifting PRE after viewing the trajectory. Do not treat red-light storage and later platoon release as the intervention: the proposed A_OPEN treatment activates R demand after540, without deliberate advance queueing.

## 3. State1-type M event and attribution

A witness requires an independently reviewed, **fully qualifying locked P episode** in the merge core after meaningful R exposure, with the exact local immediately preceding reference, population, density, persistence and attribution gates met. A fully qualifying locked S episode with corroborating M trajectory/spatial structure may also be reviewed as a conservative State1-compatible positive because P and S are not mathematically guaranteed nested; its distinction from primary-P success must be explicit. Candidate A strengthens spatial corroboration. L-only, Candidate C, isolated low-speed vehicles, lower E1 flow alone and an unqualified numerical candidate are insufficient. The first qualifying low bin must start in the fixed primary window **[720,1440)** and the locked episode must confirm **before t=1500**; R must still enter the merge during qualification. A late post-source or clearance-only event is reported but is not a witness. Full [0,2700) observations remain visible; do not shift the primary window or event deadline after outcomes. Do not alter P/S/L/A/C thresholds, reference, exclusion or persistence after observing results. No capacity drop is required; a measured discharge change is supporting only.

Review M-specific cell/lane 30 s speed ratios, absolute speeds, slow-vehicle simultaneity, M and M+R densities, unique identities, upstream/neighbor/downstream time sequence, recovery/censoring, native E1 occupancy/flow, M-only downstream passage and local/full-route travel time. Exclude an independently established downstream-origin tailback, insertion/backlog starvation, direct mainline TLS signal, mapping/parser gap or geometry-source artifact as the event explanation. A single downstream detector cannot replace full spatial/trajectory evidence. An unresolved alternative, onset reference or matching issue keeps the pair `NOT_EVALUABLE`, even if nominal qRamp is high.

The valid outcome is labelled `STAGE6_EXPLORATORY_WITNESS` with an evidence ledger and the old method labels alongside it. It supports an R-admission-associated deterioration in this synthetic scenario. Added total freeway volume, merging, urban-TLS platoons and microscopic stochastic changes are not separately identified by this two-arm comparison. Do not claim estimated capacity, breakdown probability or optimized qMain/qRamp.

Record scheduled/planned, actual inserted, late/never-inserted, actual depart time, insertion delay, arrived, unfinished at2700 and last location for M/R/U/X; for R0 write explicit zeros, not missing. Reconcile route, tripinfo, vehroute, FCD and native lanechange IDs, and retain all failed/anomalous vehicles. Report per-interval M core/downstream and R auxiliary/through-lane exposure with exact denominator and 1 Hz boundary uncertainty. Audit network and lane mapping, 0–2700 FCD labels, E1/E2 coverage, relevant TLS states, source backlog and downstream receiving space. A runtime success code is not a data-integrity or witness PASS.

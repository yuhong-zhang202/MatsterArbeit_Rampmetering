# A/B/C follow-up after a witness — design outline only

**Not an executable card or a controller-setting selection.** Design preparation may begin after a valid Stage 6 seed17 R-admission witness; B/C execution remains conditional on a satisfactory matched seed23 robustness pair plus separate scientific review and authorization. Demand selection stops at the witness; never search for a prettier qMain/qRamp or tune B/C after reading their outcomes. The witness's A_OPEN delayed-R720 arm becomes A under the same qMain, M/U/X, R activation [540,1500), seed17, network, behavior, urban TLS, horizon and measurement contract. Its raw is reused; do not rerun A for symmetry.

Propose exactly one moderate B metering program and one stronger C program **prospectively** in a later design, with controller phase/actuation semantics, matched inputs, arrival/merge exposure and urban-cost measures fixed before either run. Historical B/C programs (22G/3y/35r and 12G/3y/45r) are evidence that restriction can build ramp queues and urban harm, but their old qMain3199.2, R from t0 outcomes are not matching treatments for a new delayed-demand witness; their timings are not automatically adopted. No ALINEA tuning or formal override design occurs here.

Desired exploratory chain, each leg requiring evidence rather than a ranking score:

- **A:** the witness has a qualifying M deterioration after actual R merge exposure; ramp/urban cost is reported, not assumed zero.
- **B:** compared at matching conditions, M operation improves under the locked event/spatial and M-specific performance evidence; the ramp queue and U side remain interpretable without material insertion/censoring or source artifacts. A mere reduced R throughput is not sufficient protection evidence.
- **C:** stronger restriction is verified in actual R admission and increases ramp storage/internal/shared occupation; whether M protection persists or reverses is measured, not stipulated. For the thesis-motivating urban trade-off, demonstrate an R-linked U obstruction or U harm with vehicle/time/space evidence beyond a ramp-only queue. Nominal red time or ramp queue length alone does not establish urban harm.

For each arm reconcile M/R/U/X lifecycle, actual merge entries, unfinished vehicles and censoring; compare M speed/density/event, M-only downstream passage, travel time and time loss; report ramp storage and shared/internal queue footprints, U wait/system time and source delays; audit downstream/TLS/geometry alternatives. Preserve negative, mixed and unknown outcomes. Do not infer pure merging friction or a general sweet spot from three exploratory treatments.

**Strong Stage 6 closeout requires a satisfactory matched seed23 robustness pair and all five independently reviewed legs:** (A) credible technical/model/measurement chain; (B) a bounded low-R versus higher-R pair showing no adjudicated low-R sustained M congestion and a post-exposure, merge-related locked State1-type M event; (C) a meaningful M protection response under one fixed moderate metering arm; (D) stronger restriction with increased ramp occupation/queue **and independently supported urban U obstruction or harm**, linked by realized exposure and vehicle/time/space evidence; (E) relevant technical, source, downstream, geometry and measurement alternatives independently assessed, with **no unresolved plausible alternative explanation** for the observed chain. Ramp-only queue growth is exploratory partial evidence and cannot close the urban externality leg or O2. A skipped or contradictory seed23 pair leaves reproducibility unresolved and prevents strong closeout. If any leg fails or is unknown within the budget, record Stage 6 PARTIAL/NOT_RESOLVED or a specifically bounded negative, then STOP. No formal experiment starts or protocol freeze follows automatically.

Witness demand values demonstrate existence only. The later formal thesis design must independently choose demand ranges, seed count, metrics, uncertainty method, controllers and any optimization criterion, with a separately frozen `docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md`. Neither this outline nor a successful Stage 6 witness promotes exploratory parameters to formal settings.

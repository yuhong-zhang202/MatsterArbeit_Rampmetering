## Evidence and decision order

All arms must report planned, inserted, arrived, unfinished and censored identities by M/R/U/X; actual departure/insertion delay; first and cumulative unique R auxiliary and through-lane entries by the **same inclusive cutoffs t=1440, 1500 and 2700 s**; controller TLS trace; M passage/completion; and the complete fixed observation windows. The primary M comparison covers both mainline lanes and all predefined merge-core cells 13–17 during `[540,1500)`. Show `[0,540)` as pre-activation context and `[1500,2700)` as a **mandatory late-tail/clearance check**. Report delayed R passage explicitly. A claimed B or C freeway benefit fails if its apparent active-window gain is offset by later M harm; unresolved R/U insertion or unfinished identities make the affected comparison ineligible until censoring is accounted for. Delayed R exit alone is not a failure. Report complete 30 s bins for the whole area and the run-specific actual R entry chronology, without choosing new cells or windows after seeing outcomes. Reconcile every decisive M/R/U exposure, speed, travel-time, queue and cost measure independently to per-vehicle FCD, vehroute and tripinfo raw where applicable, with identical cohort, time and spatial definitions; discrepancies are `NOT_EVALUABLE` until resolved. FCD and E1 have different coverage: per-vehicle route-consistent crossing reconstruction is needed when the 20 m auxiliary E1 misses R vehicles that change lanes earlier. Verify source receiving space, geometry/lane map, detector coverage, downstream tailback and direct TLS/urban effects. Report P/S/L, Candidate A/C and 0.85 reference unchanged as **diagnostics**.

**Step 0 — R0 comparator preparation.** Static input/provenance checks must make it possible to test the R0 comparison, while actual pre-treatment trajectory equality is decided after A has run. The historical `NOT_EVALUABLE`/warning status is disclosed, not converted to clean normality. Adequate M exposure, complete lifecycle and absence of an identified common artifact are necessary; if existing low-speed patterns cannot be separated from the proposed A response at matched locations/times, A is not supported.

**Step 1 — A/open (first new start, only after separately authorized prelaunch).** Require realized R merge exposure and event ordering. Compare M-only fixed same-time/lane/cell speed with simultaneous M vehicle counts and density/occupancy/trajectory structure versus R0. A candidate sustained local response should cover more than one complete 30 s bin; examine the full preselected merge-area and non-candidate windows, not only the worst episode. Cross-check M travel time/timeLoss and M-only downstream passage. A single-vehicle dip, R suppression in mixed E1, or a low speed-ratio without populated M response is insufficient. If R exposure fails, the comparison is technically `NOT_EVALUABLE`; if exposure is adequate but no sustained incremental M pressure is identified, A fails and **stop without B/C**. If source/insertion/downstream/measurement effects offer a material alternative, report `NOT_EVALUABLE` and stop.

**Step 2 — B/moderate (second new start, only if A is supported).** Compare B with A at exactly the same M/R/U/X input and fixed time/lane/cell. Require a consistent directional M improvement on populated local speed/density/trajectory evidence and an independent M-only travel-time or timeLoss/passage measure; report effect sizes and 1 s crossing-time bounds, not just signs. Require a real but manageable R queue: measure anchored extent/count/duration against mapped available physical storage, U complete-cohort insertion wait and network residence, unfinished U/R, and shared-link U impediments. **Predeclared exploratory cost rule:** the R queue stays within mapped available storage with no sustained shared-link U obstruction; the complete U cohort has no corroborated degradation against A across external insertion wait, network residence and unfinished identities. Ambiguous or mixed U/storage indicators give `NOT_IDENTIFIED`, not a B PASS. Near-equal means alone do not establish statistical or practical equivalence; no new numeric tolerance is invented. If M benefit is absent/mixed or the queue/U cost rule is not supported, **stop without C**.

**Step 3 — C/stronger (third and final new start, only if B is supported).** Against B, require materially larger R queue extent/count/duration and/or internal/shared occupation, plus corroborated U deterioration in complete-cohort external insertion wait, network residence or unfinished identities. Keep those components and censoring separate, and check source/TLS/downstream/measurement alternatives for the observed cost. Spatial blockage evidence is required **only** for a physical queue-spillback claim; queue/occupation plus U cost can support the broader cost pattern without proving spillback. Check M-only local and route measures for retention of B's protection and quantify any additional benefit; if protection is lost or the added cost is not identified, the full chain fails. If C yields a large extra M improvement, report it explicitly rather than asserting that stronger metering has only marginal freeway value.

- If all three legs are supported in this one full-network chain, with no material artifact and independent engineering/data/scientific post-run review, Stage 6 may be closed **for the user-adopted exploratory phenomenon-existence purpose only**. It would establish a bounded synthetic-model demonstration, not confirmed State1, empirical calibration, real-world validity, optimal meter settings, formal thesis parameters, or Robert-confirmed scientific scope. The empty `docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md` remains unchanged. If any leg fails, retain Stage 6 `PARTIAL` and report the bounded negative/inconclusive result rather than expanding the search.

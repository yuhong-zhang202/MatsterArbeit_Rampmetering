## A. Clean high-mobility reference versus exploratory witness control

The earlier ramp-induced validation plan and Luna execution specification require every fixed PRE/control row to pass their high-mobility screen before the labels `CONTROL_READY_FOR_TRANSITION_CARD_REVIEW` and `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED_EXPLORATORY` can be assigned. That clean-reference requirement is stricter than the narrow Stage 6 existence question. The old rule and historical F5 outcomes remain unchanged.

The later `WITNESS_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md` already states that a low-R arm need not pass every normal/reference screen row. Its `LOW_R_BACKGROUND_ACCEPTABLE` requirement instead asks for adequate M exposure and lifecycle integrity, no established sustained self-congestion, and adjudication of relevant L/C warnings, long sub-0.85 patterns and alternative causes. Thus the RI3350 and PAIR3199 `NOT_EVALUABLE` findings cannot be attributed solely to accidental application of the old all-PASS screen: repeated low-ratio patterns have not been cleared as irrelevant to the low-R counterfactual. `NO_SYSTEMATIC_ARTIFACT_EVIDENCE` does not itself exclude autonomous deterioration.

If the current contract were interpreted as requiring the complete physical cause of every low-speed pattern, that would exceed what a one-case existence demonstration needs. The necessary question is whether the unresolved pattern could invalidate a matched R-on comparison at the same absolute time and place.

## B. Treatment status and a possible narrower contract

Under the current reviewed contract and exact cards, both qMain3199.2/R0 and qMain3350.4/R0 remain `NOT_EVALUABLE`, and no treatment is released. The existing historical labels, fixed screens, classifier and thresholds are preserved.

A distinct **prospective exploratory diagnostic/witness-control overlay** is scientifically defensible. At minimum it would require sufficient observed M exposure over the fixed window and complete class lifecycle, explicit R=0/low R, no locked P/S/L qualifying sustained event, no affirmative systematic technical artifact, and no clear source/downstream alternative cause. All L/C and long low-ratio observations must be retained and located, not relabelled as normal. These conditions can support a bounded test of what changes after R admission, but P/S/L negativity alone cannot prove a clean baseline or an exploratory witness.

Before any treatment run under such an overlay, the exact new contract must be written and independently scientifically reviewed, explicitly adopted by the user, and bound to a new exact treatment card with separate run authorization. The comparison must then check realized M/U/X attributes and pre-R trajectories, same absolute-time/space control evolution, actual R entry and meaningful merge exposure before deterioration, a qualifying locked P or separately justified S event, spatial/trajectory structure and alternative causes. If the control independently deteriorates at the corresponding place/time, matching fails, or the event's cause/order remains unclear, the outcome is `NOT_EVALUABLE`. Any positive case would be one exploratory R-admission-associated observation, not a formal high-mobility reference or calibrated effect. Because the R0 outcomes are already observed, the retrospective selection bias must be disclosed.

## T1: exact refusal and what it does not show

**Observed.** At decision time `t=1206 s`, the last completed and accounted simulation step was also 1206; `CA_C24` still reported phase 1 `y`, with `nextSwitch=1206.0`, so the worker predicted native phase 2 `r` for the prospective 1206→1207 motion step. No phase API request or `simulationStep` for that interval was issued. The runtime verified one controlled link `ramp_storage_0 → ramp_accel_0` via `:ramp_mid_0_0`; storage is 204.49 m, ingress internal lane 113.08 m, receiver internal lane 81.98 m. The guard observed **27 storage + 15 ingress** R vehicles; only `R_flow.1` failed. It was on `ramp_storage_0`, front position 203.4880008102335 m, speed 0.04573822948686786 m/s, `decel=4.5 m/s²`, `tau=actionStepLength=1 s`. The front-to-lane-end gap is `204.49−203.4880008102335=1.001999189766508 m`. The receiver snapshot says available and names `R_flow.0` on `:ramp_mid_0_0`, but its TraCI secure-gap result was not independently recomputed and availability does not cancel a red-transition failure.

**Observed/local arithmetic.** Candidate A `safety.py` requires every **positive-speed** storage or ingress vehicle to satisfy `g ≥ 1.1 + v·1 + v²/(2·4.5)` (plus `1e-9` comparison tolerance); only exactly zero is exempt. For `R_flow.1`, the terms are `1.1 m` inherited constant, `0.04573822948686786 m` full-speed reaction step, and `0.00023244284851037648 m` continuous braking term. Their sum is `1.1459706723353782 m`, so the margin is `−0.14397148256887027 m`. This exactly matches the saved witness. Dropping only the extra 0.1 m would still leave `1.0459706723353782 m > 1.001999189766508 m`; the mismatch involves both the constant and the extra reaction term. The constant is inherited from V9/V10, not a demonstrated official/physical minimum. The continuous braking expression is a local conservative engineering assumption, not SUMO's discrete stop calculation. Neither `tau=1` nor `actionStepLength=1` by itself proves an additional full second of unchanged-speed travel *after* the phase event; `tau` is desired car-following headway and the signal event is processed before this motion step. A double allowance of response delay is **plausible**, not proved for every state or program.

**Primary-source supported.** The official source defines a default desired junction stopline gap of 1.0 m; absent lane stopOffset returns 0, and red/yellow use their own stop-offset and braking branch ([`MSVehicle.cpp` 125, 2728–2776, 2851–2880](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/MSVehicle.cpp#L2728-L2880), [`MSLane.cpp` 3792–3800](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/MSLane.cpp#L3792-L3800)). The A07 bound input has no relevant override; the saved 1204→1206 trajectory approached about 1.0 m while yellow. The desired offset may be reduced to the available braking space in source code, so `1.0 m` is not a universal invariant or a replacement safety threshold. For this type the yellow/red `stopDecel` expression selects at least the normal 4.5 m/s² with the unoverridden yellow-min-decel default 3.0 ([`MSVehicle.cpp` 2738–2743](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/MSVehicle.cpp#L2738-L2743), [`MSFrame.cpp` 506](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/MSFrame.cpp#L506)). Default semi-implicit Euler and 1 s step are source/config supported ([`MSFrame.cpp` 364–368](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/MSFrame.cpp#L364-L368)); its `brakeGapEuler` and `maximumSafeStopSpeedEuler` are discrete calculations ([`MSCFModel.cpp` 76–98, 849–871](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/cfmodels/MSCFModel.cpp#L849-L871)), with Krauss `stopSpeed` using action-step headway ([`MSCFModel_Krauss.cpp` 101–107](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/cfmodels/MSCFModel_Krauss.cpp#L101-L107)). This is different algebra from the custom full-speed reaction plus continuous-distance formula; no equivalence or dominance proof has been established.

**Engineering inference / unverified.** The positive-speed guard failure can coexist with native near-1 m braking because they encode different sufficient conditions. The unexecuted red step might brake and stop; it might also expose another transition hazard. Neither outcome is observed. Native source may permit an approaching vehicle to proceed if it **cannot** brake before the stop line: the stopping branch requires `canBrakeBeforeStopLine` ([`MSVehicle.cpp` 2871–2907](https://github.com/eclipse-sumo/sumo/blob/v1_26_0/src/microsim/MSVehicle.cpp#L2871-L2907)). Thus native red handling is not a blanket certificate for an abruptly imposed red.

## T3: two candidate contracts, **Proposed only**

**A1 — Native-consistent transition contract (preferred for further design review, not implementable yet).** Apply only to the pinned SUMO 1.26/Krauss/semimplicit Euler/1 s/unchanged geometry/type/phase-program state, with no speed/signal safety overrides. Delegate *vehicle speed choice and stopping response* to SUMO, but independently require: exact controlled-link/program/phase event identity; complete storage, ingress, path/receiver/foe enumeration or bounded exclusion; a source-derived check that every affected approach vehicle is in SUMO's brake-before-stopline regime at pending red (including actual stopOffset, deceleration and route, rather than `g≥1.1+v+v²/2b`); and continuing post-step phase/crossing/collision/teleport/hard-deceleration surveillance. The exact eligible-state predicate and treatment of vehicles already committed across the link require separate formal derivation from tagged source and XML. **Unverified:** whether `R_flow.1` is eligible and safe at the rejected red step, whether all future phase boundaries satisfy eligibility, and actual service. Edge cases: incoming new R, leaders on the internal lane, yellow-entering vehicle unable to brake, unlisted crossing, non-R on link, modified SUMO parameters. Offline first: adversarial state/phase/coverage fixtures including zero and low-positive speed, approach exactly at brake limit, leader/receiver blockage, unknown type/link, stale `nextSwitch`; reproduce tagged native branch inputs without executing motion. Then, only after a new Issue and three-role gate, a fresh-identity R300/S17 minimal technical smoke should observe the first red transition under a newly reviewed guard, preserving all warnings and stopping on anomaly. That smoke is **transition safety**, not rate qualification or a resurrection of A07.

**A2 — Revised independent prospective guard (alternative, not numerically specified).** Keep a vehicle-level stop-distance witness independent of observed native outcomes, but derive a **discrete-time** bound from the exact event ordering and 1 s dynamics; distinguish desired stop offset, reaction/decision interval and discrete braking distance. An indicative structure is `observed gap ≥ reviewed clearance + worst-case advance before braking + discrete stopping distance`, with each term's definition and bound to be established from tagged source and the project's protected geometry; it is **not** an approved inequality with values. Also retain A1's identity, ingress, controlled-link and receiver checks. The 1.1 m constant, automatic full `v·1` after begin-step red and continuous `v²/(2b)` cannot be copied as if source certified. Conservative guards may deny otherwise native-safe transitions and destroy service; relaxing them without source/empirical grounding may allow late-red hazards. Offline proof obligations include algebraic dominance over all enumerated positive-speed states, equality/numerical tolerance, emergency-vs-normal decel, update mode, first red step and future ingress. Then the same separately authorized new-identity minimal smoke; full service still requires later separate qualification. **Unverified:** valid clearance/reaction terms, whether a nontrivial A2 passes the A07 boundary, worst-case over random braking/leader interactions, and feasibility across 300–900.

**Engineering recommendation (Proposed).** Prefer A1 as a *safety-responsibility decomposition to investigate*, because exact native speed choice and stopline state are already implemented in tagged SUMO while the inherited 1.1 m+continuous expression has no demonstrated necessity. This does **not** mean delete the guard or accept native red behavior as certified. A1 presently lacks a completely derived eligibility predicate and a validated event/state coverage proof, so the current HOLD cannot be lifted; if those cannot be established, report both candidates unresolved and keep STOP. A2 remains a viable researchable alternative if an independent discrete bound can be derived without an arbitrary replacement margin. No Candidate B/fallback or formal-study action follows automatically.

## Time, actuation and measurement contract

For ALINEA mode, `t=600` has zero credit and an initial 900 veh/h command. One-second credit scheduling makes `[600,601)`, `[601,602)`, `[602,603)` red and the first green `[603,604)`. The requested rate is updated at `t=630,660,...,4170` from the two downstream E1 detectors' *previous complete* `[t-30,t)` occupancy percentages. Exactly 119 feedback updates are expected; `t=4200` is terminal observation, not a command. Credit persists across updates; each scheduled green consumes a slot even with no waiting vehicle. The meter remains active through `[3000,4200)`. The no-op mode never sends a traffic-light state command and logs the t=630 E1 readout so the S2 run can check its `[600,630)` alignment against XML.

The sole controlled connection is `ramp_storage_0` to `ramp_accel_0` via `:ramp_mid_0_0`, TLS `ramp_mid` link 0. Actual boundary events are measured as a vehicle ID's first appearance on that internal lane from one one-second snapshot to the next, with crossing bracket `[t,t+1)`. This is a one-second bracket, not a sub-second passage time. A vehicle on the internal lane after a later red state is not by itself classified as a red crossing; the commanded and observed state during its crossing bracket is logged. Merge entry and lane change are separate events.

Technical **candidate** service qualification: for a scheduled green, the true front vehicle on `ramp_storage_0` must be within 1.1 m of the 204.49 m lane end, move at <0.1 m/s, and have internal-lane downstream rear clearance at least its actual length plus minGap. This is checked before the slot, without skipping a faster lead vehicle to select a stopped follower. Across the run, at least 20 such slots and unique front-vehicle crossing in at least 90% are proposed as the engineering service gate; any red, multiple or wrong-front bracket crossing fails. Fewer than 20 is `NOT_SUFFICIENTLY_TESTED`; empty slots and downstream-blocked slots are recorded without treating them as algorithm failure. The 1.1 m threshold covers observed historical B22 red-stop positions near lane end; that historical observation does **not** prove a one-second green will discharge them. The 90% criterion is an engineering tolerance for this test, not a literature-calibrated research threshold. It requires independent release review before ALINEA execution.

The runner writes `controller_steps.csv` (every `[t,t+1)` command, observed state, slot/credit, true front, stopline gap, downstream clearance, actual crossing IDs and red-bracket flags), `feedback_updates.csv` (decision/application time, interval bounds, detector IDs, occupancy, interval vehicle counts, target/gain and before/raw/clipped rates), `service_windows.csv` (120 s diagnostics plus cumulative gate), and `vehicle_metadata.csv` (each departed ID's class, departure step, type, actual length and minGap). It also preserves the baseline SUMO FCD, lanechange, tripinfo, vehroute, E1/E2, queue, TLS and summary outputs under a new raw directory. Whether the no-op trajectory is neutral, E1 intervals align, stopline brackets capture every crossing, and a one-second pulse serves the qualified front vehicle remain **unverified until the relevant S2/S3 gates**.

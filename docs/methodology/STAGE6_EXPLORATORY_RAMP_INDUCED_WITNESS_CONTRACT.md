## 3. Control side: comparator eligibility, not normality

Record the following before considering the R=0 raw as a candidate comparator:

1. Adequate **observed** M exposure in the fixed comparison interval, merge core, relevant upstream and downstream regions, including complete cell/lane time labels and unique M identities; nominal qMain alone does not suffice.
2. Complete scheduled/inserted/departed/arrived/unfinished lifecycle and route/tripinfo/FCD reconciliation for M/U/X; R is an explicit zero, not missing. Retain insertion delay and any anomaly.
3. Locked P/S/L have no qualifying sustained event. Preserve every L/C warning, sub-0.85 cell-bin, failed normal-screen row, and its time, lane, cell, density, population and spatial context. P/S/L negativity does not certify a normal or congestion-free state.
4. No affirmative systematic parser, lane-map, geometry or measurement defect; no identified source starvation, direct mainline TLS restriction or downstream receiving blockage explaining the relevant comparison. The cross-control `NO_SYSTEMATIC_ARTIFACT_EVIDENCE` finding supports this narrow check but is not proof that all physical alternatives are absent.

Passing these checks yields only `EXPLORATORY_CONTROL_COMPARATOR_AVAILABLE`. Existing low-speed warnings do **not** disappear and are not automatically adjudicated as harmless. They become explicit potential confounders in the matched event analysis. If observed control data already establish a sustained collective State1-type M episode at the relevant time and place, or a material technical/source/downstream defect, the comparator is ineligible for a witness claim. If the role of a warning cannot be resolved for a proposed treatment event, the **pair** is `NOT_EVALUABLE`, even when the control was available for a diagnostic start.

- `STAGE6_EXPLORATORY_RAMP_INDUCED_WITNESS`: both arms and matching are valid; realized R exposure precedes a qualifying locked M event; the event is materially additional to the same-time/location R=0 record; and relevant alternatives are cleared by independent scientific review. Stop demand scanning after one witness.

- `NOT_EVALUABLE`: relevant control warnings cannot be distinguished from treatment onset, matching/exposure/order/reference is unresolved, an alternative cause remains material, or technical/data coverage is inadequate. Retain all observations and do not convert uncertainty into a negative result.

Every result stops this pair's execution pathway. Any other candidate, seed, controller or retry needs a separate decision, review and authorization. Preserve the historical control labels and formal-protocol status in every report.

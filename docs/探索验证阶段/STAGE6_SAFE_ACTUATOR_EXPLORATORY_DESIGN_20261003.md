# Stage 6 safe rate-capable actuator: exploratory design record

Date: 2026-10-03. Status: **in design review; no V10 run or release yet**. This record implements the direction explicitly authorized in D-017. Stage 6 remains PARTIAL and `docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md` remains empty and unfrozen. The older V8/V9 raw, cards, receipts, and scientific dispositions remain unchanged.

## Existing-data diagnosis

At the current M3600/R900 operating point, V9 safely served 556 unique ramp vehicles but did not track its feedback command. In the fixed `[1200,3000)` period, the mean command was 1070.866 veh/h and 282 front vehicles crossed (564 veh/h) while storage was continuously occupied. Independent raw-log reconciliation found a median six-second inter-crossing headway; the next front became stopped and within 1.1 m of the stop line after five to eight seconds. This identifies the **stopped-front guard in V9** as a major bottleneck in that seed. It does not establish a universal physical rate ceiling. Source: `data/processed/stage6_safe_actuator_feasibility_20261003_v1/FEASIBILITY_NOTE.md` and hash-bound JSON in the same directory.

## Required technical gate before the first run

For a due slot, identify the true storage front and every follower. The moving-front branch requires a route-aware TraCI `getLeader` lookup covering the compiled internal connector and acceleration lane, a `getSecureGap` test at the front's conservative next-step upper speed against the actual leader speed and emergency deceleration, and separate internal-lane rear clearance with the same bumper-gap convention. A complete covered lookup with no leader is a valid clear path; missing coverage or an unmapped occupied internal lane closes the branch. All followers must have enough room to stop under their actual normal deceleration. The stopped-front case retains the V9 geometry check. Never alter vehicle speed, braking or `speedMode` to make the signal pass.

After a one-second green `[t,t+1)`, inspect the actual stop-line crossing bracket. If exactly the intended front crossed, recompute the normal-braking stop distance of every remaining storage vehicle before setting red for `[t+1,t+2)`. This **post-green** check uses the observed `t+1` speed `v` and `1.1 m + v·1 s + v²/(2·normalDecel)` for a moving vehicle; the **pre-green** check retains its separate `(v+a)` one-step acceleration envelope. The post-green check must not assume another green-acceleration step when the next signal is red. If no vehicle crossed and the front can still stop normally, set red and record an empty green with spent credit. If no crossing occurred and a safe red is impossible, terminate the technical run **before the next simulation step**. A wrong-front or multiple crossing fails the safety gate. Never extend green automatically and never force an unsafe red. These are fail-closed technical conditions, not successful metering outcomes.

The exact implementation/card requires simulation-engineer verification and independent scientific review before one S17 technical pilot. The data analyst must be able to reconcile requested feedback credit, due attempts, actual signal, unique stop-line crossings, safety interventions, missing or censored vehicles, and the same 238 detector/XML lane windows. The first pilot asks whether the actuator is safe and can serve a rate it commands under sustained demand. It is not an optimization sweep and does not predetermine freeway benefit.

## Proposed predeclared rate-tracking screen

For each of the six complete 300 s windows `[1200,1500)` through `[2700,3000)`, report integrated command `C=Σr(t)/3600` vehicles, unique actual stop-line crossings `A`, opening/closing credit, dropped credit, second-by-second storage queue, and downstream receiving obstruction. A window is rate-test eligible exactly when the storage lane has at least one R vehicle at **every** decision second in that window. Receiver obstruction, front not ready, and failed safety decisions remain in the denominator and are reported as explanatory outcomes; none is used to select favorable windows. An eligible window passes if `C>0` and `|A-C|/C ≤ 0.10`. **At least one window must be eligible, and every eligible window must pass** before the policy can be copied to another seed. The one-second event bracket and credit boundary are reported separately, not used to relax the threshold after the fact. Report all six windows; with no eligible window, label rate capability NOT_TESTED. Keep the separate original ≥20 qualified slots and ≥90% crossing-of-qualified-slot service gate, with qualified meaning a due slot with a queued, unobstructed, safety-accepted front, whether moving or stopped. The final card and exact code still require independent scientific review before launch.

## Sequential execution after exact release

1. Run one new S17 actuator validation under a unique revision/raw directory, retaining all failed starts and the D-016 physical/technical/experiment accounting. Do not repeat OPEN if the same-condition V8 NOOP and current OPEN pair remain equivalent.
2. Independently check safety warnings, no red/multiple/wrong-front crossings, true front and leader coverage, credit conservation, rate screen, all 238 detector lane windows, pre-600 equivalence, and all 4050 requested vehicles including unfinished R/U and external waiting. A technically failed trial is retained, diagnosed, and repaired under a new reviewed card; it is not a negative ALINEA result.
3. If the rate and data gates pass, lock **that exact actuator/policy** and run matched control seeds 23 and 42 sequentially, checking each before the next. S17/S23/S42 results must be interpreted as three paired seed outcomes. Preserve negative or mixed mainline and urban effects.
4. After all three usable runs, compare fixed mainline state and full requested-cohort cost, realized ramp releases and queue, and shared R/U exposure separately from source waiting. Have the scientific reviewer judge the evidence, then decide Stage 6 closeout explicitly and prepare the formal experimental-stage handoff. Do not freeze or launch a formal experiment under this exploratory document.

The existing V9 550–576 veh/h service observation is a guard-specific single-seed measurement, not a reason to lower the new upper limit until a tracking gate turns green. A 550 veh/h cap would mechanically leave at least 50 of the 600 requested R vehicles upstream of the meter over this one-hour control horizon, even with perfect service.
